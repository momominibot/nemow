How to read the evidence tags
Strong = randomized trials / meta-analyses in women. Moderate = promising trials, unclear dosing. Contextual = mainly helps a specific group (e.g. PCOS, TTC, deficiency). Supplements support — they don't replace diagnosis or a Mediterranean-style diet, which has the strongest evidence of all.
