Your data,
stays with you

Everything you log in Ovuluna lives only on your device. No servers, no accounts, no tracking. Just you and your wellness.

Local-only by design

What we store

On your device only Your cycle logs, mood, water, food, sleep, and readings are saved in your browser's local storage. They never leave your phone or computer.
No accounts, no servers We do not require sign-up or login. There is no user account system, no cloud sync, and no backend database holding your information.
No tracking or analytics We do not use analytics cookies, tracking pixels, or third-party data collection. Your app usage is private to you and you alone.

Your data rights

Export for your provider Generate a summary of your wellness data to share with your doctor or healthcare provider. You choose what to include.
Clear all data anytime Remove all locally stored data from this device at any time. Once cleared, it cannot be recovered because we never had a copy.

Wellness, not medical

Please read this carefully

Ovuluna is a wellness and self-care tool. It is not a medical device, diagnostic tool, or substitute for professional medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment.

Heart rate, HRV, ovarian age estimates, cycle predictions, and reproductive stage calculations are wellness-oriented estimates and may be inaccurate. Always consult a qualified healthcare provider for medical concerns, diagnosis, or treatment decisions.
