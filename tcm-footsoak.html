Foot Soaks

WARMING RITUAL

Warm the lower jiao

Foot baths with ginger and mugwort promote circulation, calm the nervous system, and support restful sleep.

warning

Pregnant, diabetic neuropathy, open wounds, peripheral vascular disease, or severe varicose veins. Stop if dizzy or overheated.

\ud83e\uded7 Classic Warming Recipe

1
Fill basin with warm water (40\u201342\u00b0C / 104\u2013108\u00b0F). Test with your wrist first.
2
Add ingredients: fresh ginger slices (20 g) + dried mugwort (10 g) or a ginger tea bag.
3
Soak 15\u201320 min until mild warmth rises and a light sweat appears on the forehead.
4
Dry feet thoroughly, massage Yongquan (KI1) briefly, then put on warm socks.

\ud83e\udd16 Why this helps ovaries

Cold and stagnation in the lower body is linked in TCM to menstrual discomfort and poor pelvic circulation. Warming the feet draws blood downward, relaxes the nervous system, and may improve sleep quality \u2014 all supportive of hormonal balance.
