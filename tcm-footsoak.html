Foot Soaks

WARMING RITUAL

Warm the lower jiao

Foot baths warm the lower body, promote circulation, calm the nervous system, and support restful sleep.

\ud83e\uded7 Classic Warming Recipe

1
Fill basin with warm water (38\u201342\u00b0C / 100\u2013108\u00b0F). Test with your wrist or elbow first.
2
Add ingredients: fresh ginger slices (20 g) + dried mugwort (10 g). Simmer herbs in water for 15\u201320 min, strain, then pour into basin.
3
Soak 15\u201320 min in the evening, 3\u20135 times per week. Stop when mild warmth rises.
4
Dry feet thoroughly, massage Yongquan (KI1) briefly, then put on warm socks. Avoid cold drafts.

Common Foot-Soak Herbs

Ginger Moderate for warmth/sleep

Warms channels and disperses cold. Studies suggest improved peripheral warmth, sleep, and circulation.

\u26a0\ufe0f Pregnancy contraindicated

Warms channels, stops bleeding, dispels cold-damp. Often used in moxibustion and foot soaks.

Invigorates Blood and relieves menstrual pain. Avoid with anticoagulants.

\ud83e\udd16 Why this helps ovaries

In TCM, cold and stagnation in the lower body is linked to menstrual discomfort and poor pelvic circulation. Warming the feet draws blood downward, relaxes the nervous system, and may improve sleep quality \u2014 all supportive of hormonal balance.
