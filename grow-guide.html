Your Fertility Journey

Evidence-based tools for trying to conceive

Understanding your cycle is the first step. Most couples conceive within 6 months of regular unprotected intercourse, and about 85% within one year i. Tracking your fertile window, understanding ovulation signs, and supporting overall health can help.

🔍 Your Fertile Window

Pregnancy is possible only during the 6 days ending on ovulation day. Sperm can survive up to 5 days in the reproductive tract, while the egg is viable for about 12-24 hours after ovulation i.

👀 Ovulation Signs to Watch

Cervical mucus: Egg-white consistency (clear, stretchy) signals peak fertility i. Basal body temperature: Rises 0.5-1°F after ovulation (use a BBT thermometer first thing in the morning). Ovulation pain (mittelschmerz): Mild one-sided cramping around ovulation. Libido: Often increases around ovulation.

💡 Track cervical mucus daily in Ovuluna. The transition from sticky/creamy to egg-white is your most reliable natural fertility signal.
💪 Preconception Health

Folic acid: 400 mcg daily starting at least 1 month before conception reduces neural tube defects by 50-70% i. Weight: Both underweight and overweight can affect fertility; a BMI of 18.5-24.9 is associated with shortest time to pregnancy i. Alcohol & smoking: Both reduce fertility in women and men; quitting smoking improves fertility within months.

🥗 Nutrition for Fertility

The fertility diet pattern from the Nurses' Health Study II: high monounsaturated fats, vegetable protein, high-fiber low-glycemic carbs, full-fat dairy, and iron from plant sources or supplements was associated with lower risk of ovulatory infertility i. Key foods: leafy greens, berries, fatty fish (omega-3), walnuts, Greek yogurt, lentils, eggs, and complex carbs.

🧘 Stress & Conception

Chronic stress can disrupt the hypothalamic-pituitary-adrenal axis and affect ovulation. A large prospective study found that women with higher self-reported stress took longer to conceive i. Mind-body interventions (mindfulness, yoga, CBT) may improve pregnancy rates in women undergoing fertility treatment i.

🏥 When to Seek Help

Under 35: If not pregnant after 12 months of regular unprotected intercourse. 35 or older: If not pregnant after 6 months (fertility declines more rapidly after 35) i. Any age: Seek help sooner if you have irregular cycles, known PCOS/endometriosis, history of PID/STIs, multiple miscarriages, or if your partner has known fertility issues.

⚠️ Ovuluna is a wellness tracking tool, not a fertility diagnostic. If you have concerns about fertility, talk to your OB-GYN or a reproductive endocrinologist.
👨 Male Factor Matters Too

About 30-40% of infertility involves male factors. Sperm quality can be improved by: avoiding hot tubs/saunas, quitting smoking, reducing alcohol, maintaining healthy weight, eating antioxidant-rich foods (zinc, selenium, vitamin C/E), and avoiding tight underwear. A semen analysis is the first test for male fertility i.
