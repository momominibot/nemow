Coherent Breathing
Follicular · Energy & Focus
Slow, rhythmic breathing at 6 breaths per minute. This maximizes heart rate variability (HRV), balances your autonomic nervous system, and boosts the energy and clarity your follicular phase naturally supports i. HRV is a key marker of stress resilience and overall wellness in women i.
5s Inhale
5s Exhale
6 breaths/min
💡

Guided: Sit comfortably, shoulders relaxed. Inhale through your nose for a slow count of 5, feeling your belly expand. Exhale gently through pursed lips for 5. Let the breath be smooth and even, like ocean waves.

4-7-8 Breathing
Luteal · PMS & Calm
A calming breath pattern developed by Dr. Andrew Weil. The extended exhale and hold activate your parasympathetic nervous system, reducing anxiety and easing the tension and irritability common in the luteal phase i.
4s Inhale
7s Hold
8s Exhale
💡

Guided: Place the tip of your tongue behind your upper front teeth. Inhale quietly through your nose for 4 counts. Hold your breath for 7 counts. Exhale completely through your mouth, making a whoosh sound, for 8 counts. Repeat 4 cycles to start.

📦
Box Breathing
Anytime · Stress Relief
Also known as square breathing, used by Navy SEALs to stay calm under pressure. Equal four-part breathing creates a steady, meditative rhythm that lowers cortisol and sharpens focus i. Perfect before a stressful meeting or when you feel overwhelmed.
4s Inhale
4s Hold
4s Exhale
4s Hold
💡

Guided: Visualize tracing the four sides of a square. Inhale as you trace the top (4 counts). Hold tracing the right side (4). Exhale down the left side (4). Hold along the bottom (4). Each full square is one cycle. Aim for 5–10 squares.

Body Scan Meditation
Evening · Sleep & Release
A progressive relaxation practice that moves awareness through each body part, releasing tension and preparing you for deep sleep. Especially helpful during the luteal phase when progesterone rises and sleep can become more restless i. Quality sleep is foundational for hormonal balance i.
10m Full body
→ Head to toe
🌙 Best before bed
💡

Guided: Lie down comfortably. Bring attention to the top of your head, slowly scanning down — forehead, eyes, jaw, neck, shoulders, arms, chest, belly, hips, legs, feet. At each area, notice tension without judgment, then breathe into it and let it soften. Let your body feel heavy and supported by the bed.

Why Breathwork Works

💓
Boosts HRV. Slow breathing increases heart rate variability, a marker of autonomic balance and stress resilience i. Ovuluna can track your HRV via PPG sensor i.
🧘
Lowers cortisol. Extended exhales activate the vagus nerve and parasympathetic response, reducing the stress hormone cortisol i.
🔄
Cycle-synced benefits. Matching breathwork to your phase — energizing coherent breathing in follicular, calming 4-7-8 in luteal — works with your natural hormonal rhythm rather than against it.
