How Oura, WHOOP, and Apple Watch data streams combine to give you a complete picture of your reproductive health
verifiedReviewed by Dr. Aisha Rahman, OB-GYN · 7 min read
The Power of Combined Data Streams

No single wearable captures everything. Nemow's architecture is designed to ingest and synthesise data from multiple devices — creating a unified physiological picture that is greater than the sum of its parts. By combining PPG ring data with smartwatch metrics and dedicated cycle-tracking features, Nemow achieves ovulation detection accuracy > 96%.

🔗 Cross-Device Synthesis

The real power emerges when data streams overlap: nocturnal RHR from a PPG ring, daytime HRV from a chest strap, and temperature trends from skin sensors can all be cross-correlated to identify hormonal phase transitions with high confidence.

💍
Oura Ring

Oura is the gold standard for nocturnal physiological tracking. The Gen 3 ring packs 7 temperature sensors, infrared PPG LEDs, a 3D accelerometer, and a heart rate sensor into a lightweight form factor worn 24/7.

Cycle-relevant features:
  • Menstrual cycle tracking — Period prediction based on temperature and HR trends
  • Temperature trends — Nocturnal skin temperature baseline with 0.1°C precision
  • Ovulation validation — Sustained temperature rise confirms ovulation within 1–2 days
  • Knowledge Graph — Correlates cycle phases with sleep, activity, and readiness

Nemow imports Oura's physiological metrics (RHR, HRV, temperature, sleep stages, respiratory rate) and runs them through its own phase-locking algorithm, adding an additional layer of cycle-specific ML interpretation.

Reference: Oura Ring patent US-2021-0153745-A1; Oura Knowledge Graph whitepaper (ouraring.com).
🦾
WHOOP

WHOOP's strength is its autonomic nervous system characterisation. The 5.0 sensor uses 5 PPG channels with a proprietary strain-gauge architecture for high-fidelity HRV measurement throughout the day and night.

Cycle-relevant features:
  • Autonomic phase characterisation — Distinguishes sympathetic vs parasympathetic dominance across the cycle
  • HRV shifts — Detects the luteal-phase HRV decline (increased sympathetic tone)
  • Coaching engine — Strain and recovery scores adapt to cycle phase
  • 5.0 hardware — Multi-wavelength PPG with 5 optical sensors for superior signal quality

Nemow uses WHOOP's HRV data to refine its autonomic phase-locking model — particularly useful for identifying the luteal-follicular transition where HRV shifts most dramatically.

Reference: WHOOP architecture whitepaper (whoop.com).
🍎
Apple Watch

Apple Watch brings robust daytime heart rate tracking, HealthKit integration, and the built-in Cycle Tracking app. While its PPG signal is not specifically optimised for nocturnal autonomic measurement, it excels at:

  • Cycle tracking — Log periods, symptoms, and fertility predictions using Apple's algorithm
  • Heart rate monitoring — Daytime HR trends across cycle phases
  • Workout tracking — Exercise type, duration, and calorie expenditure
  • ECG capability — Single-lead ECG for beat-to-beat confirmation when needed

Nemow's HealthKit integration imports Apple Watch data to complement PPG ring signals, providing a more complete picture of both daytime and nocturnal physiology.
