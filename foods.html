🔄 Phase-Synced Nutrition

🌱 Follicular Phase (Days 1–14)

TCM: Yin-growing phase — nourish Kidney yin, build Liver blood. Cooling to neutral, yin-nourishing foods.
Western: Support estrogen metabolism, provide building blocks for follicle & endometrium.
Key foods: Black beans, goji berries, eggs, bone broth, oysters, spinach, seaweed, tofu.
Avoid: excessive warming foods, alcohol, raw/cold foods
🥣Breakfast: Congee with goji berries, black sesame, soft-boiled egg + sautéed kale
🥗Lunch: Quinoa bowl with black beans, avocado, pumpkin seeds, ginger-sesame dressing
🐟Dinner: Steamed salmon with broccoli, shiitake mushrooms, brown rice

🌕 Luteal Phase (Days 15–28)

TCM: Yang phase — warm Kidney yang, move Liver qi. Warming, yang-nourishing foods.
Western: Support progesterone, manage blood sugar, reduce inflammation. Increase magnesium, B6, complex carbs.
Key foods: Ginger, cinnamon, walnuts, lamb, chicken, turmeric, dark chocolate, sweet potatoes.
Avoid: cold/raw foods, smoothies, ice water, excess caffeine
🥣Breakfast: Warm oatmeal with cinnamon, walnuts, banana, honey + ginger tea
🍗Lunch: Chicken & roasted sweet potato bowl with chickpeas, turmeric-tahini dressing
🍲Dinner: Lamb & root vegetable stew with rosemary + sautéed Swiss chard

🩸 Menstrual Phase (Days 1–5)

TCM: Rest, replenish, gently move blood. Warming blood-nourishing soups.
Western: Replenish iron. Anti-inflammatory support. Iron-rich + vitamin C, ginger, turmeric, omega-3.
Avoid: excessive salt (bloating), excess caffeine (vasoconstriction), raw/cold foods
🍲Meal: Warming ginger-chicken soup with red dates, rice congee
🍵Drink: Ginger tea with brown sugar, warm turmeric golden milk

⚠️ Contraindications & Interactions

Blood thinners (warfarin): Caution with goji berry, dang gui, ginger (high dose)
Pregnancy: Avoid dang gui, raspberry leaf (early), licorice root (high dose)
Soy + breast cancer: 1-2 servings/day associated with reduced recurrence — discuss with oncologist
Spleen qi deficiency (TCM): Avoid all cooling/raw foods. Add ginger, cardamom, cinnamon
Kidney stones: Moderate spinach, beet greens, almonds. Pair with calcium
Gout: Limit organ meats, bone broth (excessive), sardines, oysters
Hashimoto's: Cook cruciferous vegetables. Moderate soy. Ensure adequate iodine
