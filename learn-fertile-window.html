Understanding Your Fertile Window
verifiedReviewed by Dr. Aisha Rahman, OB-GYN · 5 min read

You are naturally fertile for only about 6 days each cycle: the five days leading up to ovulation and the day of ovulation itself. Sperm can survive up to 5 days in fertile cervical mucus, while the egg is viable for only 12–24 hours after release. Timing intercourse to this window is the foundation of fertility awareness.

🔑 The Three Key Signals

1. Cervical mucus: Egg-white, stretchy, clear mucus signals peak fertility. This is your most reliable real-time sign. 2. LH surge: A rise in luteinizing hormone triggers ovulation ~24–36 hours later. OPKs detect this surge. 3. BBT shift: A sustained temperature rise of 0.3–0.5°C confirms ovulation has already happened — useful for retrospective confirmation.
