Decoding Your Discharge

Your cervical mucus changes predictably across your cycle. It is one of the most reliable fertility signals your body produces — and it tells you more than just "fertile or not." Each texture and colour provides clues about your hormonal state. i

💧 Dry / Sticky

Right after your period, many women have little to no noticeable discharge. When present, it may feel sticky or crumbly. This signals low estrogen — your body is waiting to gear up for the next ovulation. i

🥛 Creamy / Lotion-like

As estrogen begins rising in the follicular phase, discharge becomes creamy, white or yellow-tinted. It feels like lotion and may leave a sensation of moisture. This is fertile-quality mucus — a sign your body is preparing for ovulation. i

🥚 Egg-White (Most Fertile)

At peak fertility, right before ovulation, discharge becomes clear, stretchy, and slippery — like raw egg white. It can stretch an inch or more between your fingers. This is the most fertile mucus quality, helping sperm swim toward the egg. It signals your highest chance of conception. i i

⚠️ When to Check With a Doctor

Green, grey, or foamy discharge with a strong odour may indicate infection. Clumpy white discharge resembling cottage cheese (especially with itching) suggests a yeast infection. Yellow-green with a fishy odour may be bacterial vaginosis. Spotting between periods or bleeding after sex also warrants a check.
