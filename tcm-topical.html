WARMTH THERAPY

Gentle external warmth

Moxibustion, warm compresses, and herbal poultices applied to the lower abdomen support circulation and ease discomfort.

dangerous
Hard avoid
Moxibustion and strong heat over the lower abdomen are contraindicated in pregnancy. Avoid on broken skin, burns, areas with reduced sensation, and during hot flashes. Use in a ventilated space.

\ud83d\udd25 Moxibustion

Burning dried mugwort (moxa) near acupuncture points to warm channels and move qi. Best for cold-type cramps and low back ache.

1
Light a moxa stick and hold it 2\u20133 cm above the point (CV4, CV6, or SP6).
2
Move in small circles or pecking motions. Never hold still to avoid burns.
3
Continue until skin is rosy and warm, usually 10\u201315 minutes.
4
Extinguish the moxa stick fully in a glass jar. Ventilate the room.
schedule 10\u201315 min \u00b7 sync 2\u20133\u00d7/week

\ud83e\uddca Warm Compress

A simple, safe alternative to moxa. Use a warm (not hot) water bottle or microwaveable pack over the lower abdomen.

1
Fill a hot water bottle with warm water (around 45\u00b0C / 113\u00b0F).
2
Place a thin cloth between the bottle and skin.
3
Rest for 15\u201320 minutes over the lower abdomen or lower back.
4
Remove if skin becomes red, numb, or uncomfortable.

\ud83c\udf3f Herbal Poultice

A cloth pack filled with warm herbs applied to the abdomen. Ginger and mugwort are common warming choices.

1
Steep 30 g dried mugwort or ginger in hot water for 10 minutes.
2
Strain and wrap herbs in a clean cotton cloth.
3
Apply warm (test on wrist) over lower abdomen for 15 minutes.
4
Discard herbs after one use.
Stop and seek help if:
  • cancel Redness or blistering lasts >30 min
  • cancel Dizziness, nausea, or headache from smoke
  • cancel Unusual pain or burning sensation
