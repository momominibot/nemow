\ud83d\udcda What these markers mean

AMH and AFC reflect ovarian reserve. FSH/E2 reflect how hard the brain is working to stimulate the ovaries. None predict natural fertility or egg quality on their own \u2014 they estimate response to fertility treatment and help contextualize where you are relative to typical age patterns.
